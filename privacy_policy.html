2. Data we process

  • Local game progress — credits, upgrades, settings, achievements, tracks. Stored on your device only (user://). You may export a backup from Settings.
  • Optional display name & country code — for the in-game local leaderboard only; not sent to a developer server (no online account backend in v1.4).
  • Advertising data — if you watch optional rewarded ads, Google AdMob and partners may process advertising ID, IP address, device info, and ad interactions.
  • Purchases — Google Play Billing processes payments for Remove ads, Starter pack, and VIP skins. We only receive unlock/entitlement status, not full card details.

We do not require registration and do not intentionally collect real names, emails, photos, or precise GPS inside game logic.

3. Advertising

The App may show optional rewarded ads (boost, offline ×2, bonus tokens, streak repair). Core track progression does not require watching an ad.

Partners act under their own policies: Google Privacy Policy.

If you purchase Remove ads, rewarded ad placements are disabled; rewards may still be granted without watching an ad.

4. In-app purchases

Optional products: remove ads, starter pack, VIP skins. Managed by Google Play. Refunds follow Google’s policies.

5. Children

The App is intended for a general audience. If you market to children, configure AdMob as child-directed and follow Play Families policies.

6. Sharing & sale of data

We do not sell personal data. Payment and advertising providers process data as independent controllers under their terms.

7. Retention & security

Local saves remain until you clear app data, uninstall, or overwrite via import. Backups you export are under your control. We do not operate a required cloud save server; optional Play Games plugins may store a snapshot if you enable them.

8. Your choices

  • Disable ads in Settings (when allowed) or purchase Remove ads.
  • Opt out of personalized ads in device / Google settings.
  • Export or reset progress in Settings.
  • Uninstall the App to stop local processing.
  • Contact us with privacy questions.

9. International transfers

Ad and payment partners may process data in other countries under their safeguards.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change. Material changes will be reflected in the Play listing when required.
