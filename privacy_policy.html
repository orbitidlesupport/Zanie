2. Data we process

We do not require registration and do not intentionally collect real names, email addresses (except when you contact support), photos, or precise GPS location inside game logic.

3. Advertising

The App may show optional rewarded ads (for example boost, offline ×2, bonus tokens, streak repair). Core track progression does not require watching an ad.

Advertising partners act under their own policies, including the Google Privacy Policy.

If you purchase Remove ads, rewarded ad placements are disabled; equivalent rewards may still be granted without watching an ad where the App provides that option.

4. In-app purchases

Optional products may include remove ads, starter pack, and VIP skins. Purchases are managed by Google Play. Refunds follow Google’s policies.

5. Children

The App is intended for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13. If you configure the App for child-directed treatment, you must follow applicable Play Families policies and AdMob child-directed settings.

6. Sharing & sale of data

We do not sell personal data. Payment and advertising providers process data as independent controllers or processors under their terms.

7. Retention & security

Local saves remain until you clear app data, uninstall, or overwrite via import. Backups you export are under your control. We do not operate a required cloud save server; optional platform features (e.g. Play Games) may store a snapshot only if you enable them and the feature is available.
